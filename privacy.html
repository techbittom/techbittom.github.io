Summary

Techbit Office is a private tool used only by its owner. It does not collect data from the public and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Data from TikTok

When the owner connects their own TikTok account, the tool receives from TikTok: the basic profile information of that account (open ID, display name, avatar; scope user.info.basic) and permission to upload videos as drafts (scope video.upload).

How it is used

The profile information is only used to show which account is connected. The upload permission is only used to send the owner's own approved videos to the owner's TikTok drafts.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other server except TikTok's official API.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Deleting

The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time in the tool, which deletes the stored tokens, and can also remove the app's access in the TikTok app settings.
