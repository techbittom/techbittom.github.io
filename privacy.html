Summary

Techbit Office is a private tool used only by its owner. It does not collect data from the public and does not sell or share data with anyone.

Data from TikTok, YouTube and Instagram

When the owner connects their own accounts, the tool receives only what is needed to upload the owner's videos: from TikTok the basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar; user.info.basic) and permission to upload drafts (video.upload); from YouTube/Google the permission to upload videos and read the owner's channel name (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly); from Instagram the basic profile and permission to publish the owner's Reels (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish).

How it is used

Profile information is only used to show which account is connected. Upload permissions are only used to send the owner's own videos, after the owner approves each post.

Where it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any other server except TikTok's official API.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Deleting

The owner can disconnect TikTok at any time in the tool, which deletes the stored tokens, and can also remove the app's access in the settings of TikTok, Google and Instagram.
